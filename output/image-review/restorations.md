Prompt: Faithful photographic restoration of the supplied property photo. Preserve the room or building layout, furniture, bedding colours and patterns, curtains, lamps, tables, chairs, rug, windows, plants, and vehicles. Improve clarity, compression noise, midtone brightness, white balance, and highlights. Do not renovate, add amenities, change the perspective, or add text or watermarks. Output a higher-resolution image in the original proportions.

These are AI-assisted restorations, which reconstruct fine details. They are identified in descriptive alt text, and the gallery explains that photographs have been restored for clarity. They do not replace current room confirmation with the property.

The restored entrance was rejected because its sign lettering changed; the actual entrance photo remains on the website.

Mobile design reference: reviewed sixteen frames spanning the supplied 61.6-second recording. Applied generous image blocks, restrained captions anchored at bottom left, natural photo proportions, and a left-aligned hero. The source video is not included in the repository.
